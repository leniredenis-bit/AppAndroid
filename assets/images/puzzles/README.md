# 🧩 Briefing: imagens bíblicas para o Quebra-Cabeça

Obrigado por ajudar! Este documento tem **tudo o que você precisa** para gerar as imagens.
O objetivo são **12 ilustrações** de cenas bíblicas que vão virar quebra-cabeças num app
de jogos para famílias e crianças (JW Splash Games).

---

## 1. Como trabalhar

1. Use uma IA de imagens (ChatGPT/DALL·E, Gemini, Midjourney, Leonardo, Ideogram…).
2. **Cole primeiro o "Prompt-mestre" (seção 3)** para a IA entender o estilo e as regras.
3. Depois peça **uma imagem por vez**, colando o prompt da cena (seção 5).
4. Gere 2–4 versões de cada cena e escolha a melhor usando o **checklist (seção 6)**.
5. Corrija o que precisar pedindo ajustes (exemplos na seção 7).
6. Salve com o nome indicado e entregue (seção 8).

> Os prompts estão em **inglês** porque as IAs de imagem entendem melhor. Pode colar como está.

---

## 2. Regras obrigatórias (leia antes de tudo)

O app é usado por famílias Testemunhas de Jeová. Por isso:

| ❌ Nunca | ✅ Em vez disso |
|---|---|
| Representar **Deus (Jeová)** como pessoa, rosto, mão ou figura no céu | Luz suave vinda do céu, raios de sol, natureza |
| **Auréolas** (círculo de luz na cabeça) | Pessoas normais, sem brilho especial |
| **Cruzes** em qualquer lugar | Nada; simplesmente não incluir |
| **Anjos com asas** | Não incluir anjos nestas cenas |
| Violência, sangue, medo, armas em uso, cenas assustadoras | Cenas calmas, alegres, acolhedoras |
| Roupas modernas, prédios modernos, objetos anacrônicos | Roupas e construções do Oriente Médio antigo |
| Texto, letras, logotipos, marcas d'água, assinaturas | Imagem limpa, sem nenhuma escrita |
| Copiar ou imitar ilustrações de publicações (ex.: jw.org) | Arte original gerada por você |
| Nudez ou roupas reveladoras | Túnicas e mantos longos e modestos |

Outras orientações:
- **Jesus** pode aparecer (seção 5, cenas 9 e 10): homem adulto de uns 30 anos, pele morena do Oriente Médio, barba curta, túnica simples. **Sem auréola**, sem brilho especial.
- **Pessoas** com aparência do Oriente Médio antigo: pele morena/oliva, cabelos escuros.
- **Animais** sempre amigáveis e realistas no estilo do desenho (nada de mostrar dentes de forma ameaçadora).

## 3. Prompt-mestre (cole uma vez no início da conversa)

```
You are going to create a series of 12 square illustrations for a children's Bible puzzle game.
Keep the SAME art style in every image:

STYLE: warm, colorful children's storybook illustration, soft painterly digital art,
gentle lighting, rich detail spread across the WHOLE image (foreground, middle and background),
friendly expressions, vibrant but natural colors, no harsh black outlines.

COMPOSITION: square 1:1, the main subject in the center-middle area, plenty of varied details
everywhere (plants, animals, textures, clouds) so every part of the image looks different
— this is important because the image will be cut into jigsaw pieces.
Avoid large areas of a single flat color (plain sky, plain wall, plain sea).

PEOPLE: ancient Middle Eastern people with olive/brown skin and dark hair, wearing long modest
tunics and robes in earthy colors; no modern clothes or objects.

STRICT RULES (never break):
- never depict God as a person, face, hand or figure in the sky; use soft light or sunbeams instead
- no halos, no crosses, no winged angels
- no violence, weapons in use, blood or scary scenes
- no text, letters, logos, signatures or watermarks
- modest clothing only

Reply "OK" and wait for me to send each scene.
```

---

## 4. Especificações técnicas

- **Formato:** quadrado **1:1**, no mínimo **1024 × 1024 px**
- **Arquivo final:** **JPG**, qualidade ~80%, **até 350 KB** cada
  (se ficar maior, comprima em https://squoosh.app → JPG/MozJPEG, qualidade 75–80)
- **Nomes:** exatamente como na tabela da seção 5 (minúsculas, sem acento, sem espaço)

**1. Arca de Noé**
```
Scene: Noah's Ark. A huge wooden ark with a ramp, resting on green hills. Noah (older man with
a white beard) and his family gently guide pairs of animals up the ramp: elephants, giraffes,
lions, zebras, sheep, camels, rabbits, turtles, and many colorful birds flying above.
Soft morning light, a few clouds, a rainbow in the distance, flowers in the foreground.
```

**2. Jardim da criação**
```
Scene: a lush, peaceful garden at sunrise, with no people. A clear river winds through
flowering meadows, fruit trees with oranges and figs, palm trees, a waterfall in the back.
Deer, rabbits, peacocks, ducks, butterflies and hummingbirds everywhere.
Soft golden sunbeams through the trees.
```

**3. Jonas e o grande peixe**
```
Scene: Jonah, a bearded man in a simple robe, sitting on a sandy beach looking relieved and
thankful, while a huge friendly whale swims away in the turquoise sea behind him.
Seagulls, shells, rocks, gentle waves; in the far distance on a hill, the ancient walled city of
Nineveh. Bright afternoon light.
```

**4. Davi, o jovem pastor**
```
Scene: young David, a teenage shepherd boy with curly dark hair, sitting on a rock playing
a small wooden harp on a grassy hillside, surrounded by a flock of fluffy sheep and lambs.
Olive trees, wildflowers, a shepherd's staff and a sling resting beside him (not in use),
distant hills and a warm orange sunset sky with soft clouds.
```

**5. Daniel e os leões**
```
Scene: Daniel, a calm older man with a grey beard in a long robe, kneeling peacefully and
praying inside a stone den, surrounded by large lions that are lying down and resting
calmly next to him, some asleep. Soft warm light coming from an opening above.
Stone textures, straw on the floor, peaceful mood, not scary at all.
```

**6. Travessia do Mar Vermelho**
```
Scene: a great crowd of Israelite families walking on a dry sandy path between two tall walls
of deep blue sea water, with fish and seaweed visible inside the water walls.
People carry baskets and children, lead sheep, goats and donkeys. Moses, an older man with a
white beard and a staff, stands on a rock at the side. Dramatic but hopeful sky with sunbeams.
```

**7. A ovelha perdida**
```
Scene: a kind shepherd in a brown robe walking across green rolling hills, smiling, carrying
a small white lamb on his shoulders. In the background, the rest of the flock waits near a
stone sheepfold. Wildflowers, a stream, olive trees, soft late-afternoon light.
```

**8. Rute no campo de cevada**
```
Scene: Ruth, a young woman with a headscarf and long robe, gathering barley stalks into a
basket in a golden field at harvest time. Workers cutting grain in the background, bundles of
sheaves, a donkey with baskets, a small village on a hill, blue sky with fluffy clouds.
```

**9. Jesus e as crianças**
```
Scene: Jesus, a man about 30 years old with olive skin, short dark beard and a simple
cream-colored tunic (no halo, no glow), sitting under a large tree and talking warmly with a
group of happy children of different ages; one small child sits close to him. Parents stand
nearby smiling. A stone village, a well, flowers and doves in the background.
```

**10. A pesca milagrosa**
```
Scene: four fishermen in a wooden boat on the Sea of Galilee, pulling a net overflowing with
silver fish, amazed and happy. Jesus (same look as before: olive skin, short dark beard,
simple tunic, no halo) stands on the shore watching kindly. Morning mist over the water,
hills, seagulls, a second boat nearby.
```

**11. O Paraíso na Terra**
```
Scene: a joyful family of different ages having a picnic in a beautiful park-like paradise.
A lion lies peacefully beside a lamb, a child pets a friendly bear, a girl feeds a giraffe,
a boy plays with a tiger cub. Waterfalls, a lake, mountains, fruit trees and flowers of every
color, colorful birds. People in simple modern-neutral casual clothes (no logos).
Bright, happy, sunny atmosphere.
```

**12. O templo de Salomão**
```
Scene: the grand ancient temple in Jerusalem built by King Solomon, with cream stone walls,
tall bronze pillars at the entrance, golden details and a large courtyard. Many people in
colorful robes bringing baskets of fruit, doves, palm trees, a clear blue sky.
View from slightly above, full of detail.
```

## 6. Checklist de aprovação (cada imagem)

- [ ] Quadrada, nítida, sem partes deformadas (mãos, rostos, patas, olhos)
- [ ] Nenhum item da lista "❌ Nunca" (seção 2)
- [ ] **Nenhum texto ou letra** escondido na imagem
- [ ] Detalhes espalhados pela imagem toda (dá para distinguir cada pedaço)
- [ ] Mesmo estilo das outras imagens da série
- [ ] Cena reconhecível para uma criança
- [ ] Arquivo JPG ≤ 350 KB com o nome certo

---

## 7. Pedidos de ajuste prontos

```
Same image, but remove the halo / glow around the head.
Same image, but remove any text or letters.
Same image, but fix the hands and faces so they look natural.
Same image, but add more details in the sky and corners (clouds, birds, plants).
Same image, but make the animals look calmer and friendlier.
Same image, but keep the same art style as the previous images in this series.
```

---

## 8. Entrega

- Envie os 12 arquivos `.jpg` (Drive, WhatsApp como **documento** para não perder qualidade, ou e-mail).
- Se alguma cena não ficou boa depois de algumas tentativas, pode pular e avisar.

**Para quem mantém o app:** basta colocar os arquivos nesta pasta
(`assets/images/puzzles/`). Eles aparecem sozinhos no menu do quebra-cabeça, em ordem
alfabética. Não precisa mexer no código.
